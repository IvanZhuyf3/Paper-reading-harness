Under the tight focusing condition, a FDTD simulation is conducted to calculate the dependence
of SRP intensity on the molecular concentration. First, a thermal ball lens model is generated in

===== SUPPLEMENT PDF PAGE 4 =====
4

MATLAB as a matrix, where the refractive index outside the ball lens is set to the refractive
index of the DMSO medium (n 0 = 1.4793), and the refractive index inside the ball lens region is
set to different lower values (n = n0 + Δn). Then, the thermal ball lens model is imported into
FDTD as an “(n,k) material” object. A Gaussian focused light source is sent through the thermal
ball lens object and is measured with a far-field intensity analyzer, of which the output is a far-
field angular distribution of intensity. The middle 31° (~0.4 NA) of the angular distribution of
intensity is integrated, then subtracted by the corresponding 0-concentration value to determine
the SRP signal intensity.
