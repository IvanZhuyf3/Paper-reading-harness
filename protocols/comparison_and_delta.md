# Comparison and Delta

The paper is a reference trajectory, not an answer key.

Compare:
- what the human noticed/missed;
- what the authors noticed/missed;
- assumptions;
- claim burden;
- falsifiability;
- information gain;
- present-day alternatives.

Good disagreement is allowed.

At the end ask:

> What's the delta?

Keep it free-form and short. Do not manufacture a lesson if none exists.
